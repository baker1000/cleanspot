## 2. Visiting the website and the app

When you open CleanSpot, our hosting provider processes technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, requested address, browser type. This data is only used to deliver the app and to defend against attacks, and is deleted after [[number]] days.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)([[e (public body) or f (legitimate interest in secure operation)]]) GDPR.

## 3. Reports

When you submit a report, we store:

- the location (GPS position or the point you set) and its accuracy,
- category, amount, the type of hazardous material if any, and your optional comment,
- 1–3 photos and the time of the report,
- a random identifier of your device or account (see section 4).

**Photos:** photos are made smaller on your device; all metadata (EXIF) is removed in the process, e.g. GPS data, camera model and capture time. Photos only become public after a review or after [[number, default 3]] other users have confirmed the report. Please do not photograph people or licence plates.

**Publicly visible** are: location, category, amount, status, history and – after review – photos and comment. Your name or email address is never shown publicly.

**Recipients:** staff of the municipality in whose area the report lies see the full report in order to handle it. Outside the areas of participating municipalities, the community handles the report.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)([[e GDPR in connection with the municipality's waste management duties, or f GDPR for a private operator]]).

## 4. Using CleanSpot without and with an account

**Without an account:** when you first report, a random, anonymous identifier is created and stored in your browser, so you can find your reports again later. We know neither your name nor your email address.

**With an account:** we store your email address, your password (as a hash only), an optional display name and your language. To confirm your email address we send you an email via [[email delivery service]].

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (terms of use).

## 5. Confirming, clearing and bags

When you confirm a report, take it on ("I'll clear this") or mark it as cleared, we store this with your identifier and the time. The photo after clearing and the photo of bags left for pickup are stored with the location and time of capture: this is how we check that the photo was taken at the report (default: within 50 m). Municipal staff see the location of bags left for pickup in order to collect them.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

## 6. Location

The app only asks for your location when you trigger it (report, after-photo, bags, "my location" on the map, start of the pickup route). Your location is not tracked continuously and is only stored together with a report or a photo. On the map and for the pickup route it stays on your device. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your device or browser settings.

## 7. Storage on your device

CleanSpot uses no cookies. Your browser's storage (local storage and IndexedDB) holds: your sign-in or anonymous identifier, the chosen language, the last map view and – while there is no connection – reports not yet sent, including photos. This storage is strictly necessary for the function you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

## 8. Services used

- **Database, sign-in and photo storage:** [[Supabase Inc. with servers in the EU (state the region, e.g. Frankfurt) – or the municipality's own server]]. A data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) is in place with the provider.
- **Map tiles:** to show the map, your device loads map images from [[OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org) – or an own tile server]]. Your IP address is transmitted to this provider. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
- **Place search:** when you search for a place, the search term and your IP address are sent to [[Nominatim of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom; EU adequacy decision) – or an own server]].
- **Navigation and pickup route:** only when you tap a navigation or route link, openstreetmap.org or routing.openstreetmap.de (FOSSGIS e.V., Germany) opens with the coordinates of the destination or stops.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)([[e or f]]) GDPR.

## 9. Retention

Reports are kept as long as the municipality needs them for clearing and statistics: [[period, e.g. deleted or anonymised 3 years after completion]]. When you delete your account, your photos and comments are deleted; your reports remain without any link to you, because the waste may still be there. The photo files are removed from storage immediately, at the latest after about 25 hours.

## 10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR).

In the app's profile you can **download your data yourself** (a JSON file, including links to your photos) and **delete your account and your data yourself**. For anything else, write to [[email address]].

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. [[Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen, Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover]].

## 11. No automated decisions

There is no automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. Using CleanSpot is voluntary.

## 12. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The version published here applies.
