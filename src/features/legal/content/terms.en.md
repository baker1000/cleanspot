## 1. Purpose

CleanSpot is for reporting illegal waste dumps and organising their removal. Using it is free and voluntary. You can report without an account; confirming and clearing require an account.

## 2. Not an emergency service

CleanSpot is not an emergency service. In case of acute danger – e.g. leaking chemicals, fire or danger to people – call **112** (fire brigade) or **110** (police) immediately.

## 3. Safety

- **Do not touch** hazardous waste (e.g. batteries, chemicals, material that may contain asbestos, needles). Volunteers cannot take on such reports; they go directly to the municipality.
- When clearing: wear gloves, watch the traffic, do not enter private property without permission, do not lift anything that is too heavy.
